Races

Lola's Lake Waconia 2026 - 5K Live (Bib #540) @ 27:07

This was a fun racing day, and it started off a smidge chaotically haha! I admittedly forgot to account for road closures, so by the time I had to park my car I was 14 minutes walking-pace away with 7 minutes until the race began, so I ran to start my race.


I still managed to beat my PB at the time with a final time of 27:07, putting me in 81st of 527 racers, 18th of 312 female racers, and 4th among 20 - 24yo women! According to my Strava, this also at the time included my 2nd best 1/2 mile and 1 mile, 3rd best 1k time, and best 2 mile (16:58) and 5k! Rahhhh!!!!

Goldy's Run 2026 - 5K Live (Bib #6316) @ 29:25

This was my first official race, and IT WAS SO COOL! I really really loved the atmosphere and vibes, I just wish I had practiced a bit more! Either way, for a fun run it was really awesome! Since I was a bit scared and intimidated, I decided to only do the 5K, even though I've run an unofficial half-marathon before!


My final time was 29:25, putting me in 949th of 4,423 racers, 234th of 2,407 female racers, and 41st among 20 - 24yo women! According to my Strava, this also at the time included my 2nd best 5K, 3rd best 400m sprint, and best 1/2 mile (3:59), 1K (4:57), 1 mile (8:06) and 2 mile (17:03) segments! Rah!!!!

Climbing Competitions

Comp de Bloc 2026

This was my first climbing competition, and IT WAS AWESOME! Seeing all those problems was so interesting, and I got to see a lot of people I saw before at the gym again!


Admittedly I had to leave at 12:30p, so I only had 2/3 of the alloted time, so I didn't exactly place in anything, haha. This was still such a fun experience! And now that I know how these go, I'll have to go to more!


I don't have too much footage, sadly. But my friend and I had so much fun, and not to brag for him, but he won 1st place on the Intermediate category!!!
