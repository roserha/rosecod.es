About Me

I have always been in love with technology and figuring out ways to put my ideas in paper - or rather, in code! Starting with HTML and SimpleBasic at a young age, nowadays you can find me creating all sorts of projects in languages including, but not limited to, C++, C#, C, JavaScript, Rust, Java, Python, and others.


I’m also an avid mathematics fan, and adore learning about different tools to be able to help me create my projects. In addition, astronomy is another passion of mine, so feel free to send any space facts my way! 


You can also find me creating my own personal projects from time to time, so keep an eye out!

Besides that, I also love doing things outside of those three topics. I am a (very) passionate climber (bouldering, top roping, lead climbing, you name it!), love listening to music (and trying to play music in my guitar & keyboard), enjoy figure skating, running and doing trails, like playing sports such as volleyball, tennis, and pickleball, and adore taking care of my cats, Honey Eclair and Haley Luna!
